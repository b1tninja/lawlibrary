# Courts

A court sits under a government, or under the court that reviews its judgments. `court.py` is that hierarchy. `Bench` is the level (`supreme`, `appellate`, `trial`). `Division` is a docket a trial court hears.

The California stack registered so far comes from the local index, California Constitution article VI. The United States stack is registered from [federal.md](federal.md). Title 28 is not in the California Whoosh index.

| Court | Bench | Authority in the index | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supreme Court of California | supreme | article VI, section 1 vests the judicial power; section 2 sets the court | [supreme.courts.ca.gov](https://supreme.courts.ca.gov/) |
| California Courts of Appeal | appellate | article VI, section 3 | [courts.ca.gov/courts](https://courts.ca.gov/courts) |
| Court of Appeal, Third Appellate District | appellate | article VI, section 3; Sacramento County is in this district | [appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/3dca/about](https://appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/3dca/about) |
| Superior Court of California, County of Sacramento | trial | article VI, section 4, one superior court in each county | [saccourt.ca.gov](https://saccourt.ca.gov/) |

Article VI, section 11 states appellate jurisdiction. Article VI, section 6 creates the Judicial Council. Government Code sections 68900–68905 appoint the Reporter of Decisions. The phrases a lexer should mark in those sections — vesting, composition, establishment, original jurisdiction, appellate jurisdiction, and delegation — are [../lexical/clauses.md](../lexical/clauses.md).
