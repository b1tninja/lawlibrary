# Superior Court of California, County of Sacramento

Checked 2026-09-24 against the court’s own site and California judicial-branch pages. Official hosts only. Do not invent case-access URLs from this note; open the linked page before using a portal path. Do not attempt to log in to portals that require an account.

## Official homepage and authority

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Official homepage | [saccourt.ca.gov](https://saccourt.ca.gov/) ([default.aspx](https://saccourt.ca.gov/default.aspx); also [www.saccourt.ca.gov](https://www.saccourt.ca.gov/)) |
| Host | `saccourt.ca.gov` — California government domain (`.ca.gov`) |
| Who created the court | California Constitution article VI, section 4: one superior court in each county |

Article VI, section 4 (leginfo): “In each county there is a superior court of one or more judges.” The same section also places an appellate division in each superior court. The Judicial Branch states there are 58 trial (superior) courts, one in each county ([courts.ca.gov/courts/superior-courts](https://courts.ca.gov/courts/superior-courts); [courts.ca.gov/courts](https://www.courts.ca.gov/courts)).

This is Sacramento County’s superior (trial) court under that article VI structure. Broader California hierarchy notes are in [california.md](california.md).

**Login notes (no login attempted):**

- **Journal Technologies Civil/Probate Public Portal.** Court Case Search and the portal FAQ state an account is required to search by name or case number and to view or print documents. Tentative rulings and probate notes are described as login-gated on the portal.
- **Criminal eCourt portal.** Public landing page; Terms of Use must be accepted to use the portal. Account/log-in controls appear on the site; do not create an account or sign in for this research.
- **PCAS.** Create-account page states an account is needed for Family Law party/attorney document access (and for certain government Criminal/Traffic features), and that other PCAS uses do not require an account. Family document viewing still requires log-on plus a court-processed case subscription.

E-filing is a separate Journal Technologies filing site ([prod-efsp-sacramento-ca.journaltech.com](https://prod-efsp-sacramento-ca.journaltech.com/public-portal/)) for submitting documents; it is not a public case-search docket.

## Court of Appeal review

Sacramento County is in the **Third Appellate District** of the California Courts of Appeal.

Official confirmation: the Courts of Appeal district list includes Sacramento among the Third District counties ([appellate.courts.ca.gov](https://appellate.courts.ca.gov/); same list on [courts.ca.gov/courtsofappeal.htm](https://www.courts.ca.gov/courtsofappeal.htm)). The Third District’s about page lists the same 23 counties, including Sacramento ([appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/3dca/about](https://appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/3dca/about)). District home: [appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/3dca](https://appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/3dca).

Article VI, section 4 also creates an appellate division in each superior court for appeals that statute and the Rules of Court keep in the superior court (for example many limited civil, traffic, and small-claims matters). That division is not the Third District Court of Appeal.
