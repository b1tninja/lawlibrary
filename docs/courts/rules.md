# Court rules

A court rule is its own publication. `Instrument.RULE` is that kind. `Court.rules` is the list of rulebooks the court posts. Nothing in this note is downloaded. A PDF is a pointer. An HTML edition is the closer file to index later.

The California courts share one statewide book. A superior court may add local rules. A federal district does the same under the national rules.

## Sacramento

The home trial court is the Superior Court of California, County of Sacramento.

| Book | Shape | Where it is posted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| California Rules of Court | HTML, one page per rule | [courts.ca.gov/forms-rules/rules-court](https://www.courts.ca.gov/forms-rules/rules-court) |
| Local Rules, and standing orders | PDF. The court page names a complete set effective January 1, 2026, and advertises a July 1, 2026 set | [saccourt.ca.gov/local-rules/local-rules.aspx](https://saccourt.ca.gov/local-rules/local-rules.aspx) |

The indexable file is the HTML California Rules of Court. The local rules are PDF only, so they stay a pointer until the court posts HTML or plain text.

The federal trial court that sits in Sacramento is the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California. It is not a class in `court.py` yet. Its own local rule says the clerk keeps the rules as PDF on the court site. The current file is effective February 23, 2026: [caed.uscourts.gov local rules](https://www.caed.uscourts.gov/caednew/index.cfm/rules/local-rules/). The district’s boundaries are 28 U.S.C. § 84. National civil, criminal, evidence, appellate, and bankruptcy rules are the HTML set on [uscourts.gov](https://www.uscourts.gov/rules-policies/current-rules-practice-procedure).

## Registered courts

| Court | Rulebook | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supreme Court of California, Courts of Appeal, Third Appellate District | California Rules of Court | HTML |
| Superior Court, Sacramento | California Rules of Court, plus the local-rules PDF | HTML and PDF |
| Supreme Court of the United States | [Rules and Guidance](https://www.supremecourt.gov/filingandrules/rules_guidance.aspx) | HTML page, pamphlet linked from it |
| United States courts of appeals, and district courts as a group | Federal Rules of Practice and Procedure | HTML |
| Federal Circuit | Rules of practice, amendments effective December 1, 2025, posted as a PDF | PDF |
| United States Tax Court | [ustaxcourt.gov/rules](https://ustaxcourt.gov/rules), titles listed on the page | HTML |
| Court of Federal Claims | Rules amended through July 27, 2026 | PDF |

A district court’s own local rules are not the national set. The Eastern District of California is the one that covers Sacramento. Other districts post their own PDFs and are not registered here.
