# Official manuals, style guides, and Administrative Procedure Acts

Checked against official government and court pages opened for this note. Do not invent quotations or URLs from this file; open the linked page (or the local index) before quoting.

Two names collide as “APA.” The American Psychological Association publication manual is not a government publication and is not a source here. The Bluebook (*A Uniform System of Citation*) is likewise not a government publication and is skipped below except where a court rule names it.

### Federal

The OLRC editorial note on Subchapter II states that the provisions were originally enacted by the act of June 11, 1946, ch. 324, 60 Stat. 237, popularly known as the Administrative Procedure Act, and were later incorporated into that subchapter and chapter 7 of title 5. The United States Code title file for this project is not downloaded; use the OLRC pages above (or a govinfo United States Code package) for the text.

### 2. California Style Manual

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title | California Style Manual (*A Handbook of Legal Style for California Courts and Lawyers*) |
| Publisher | Reporter of Decisions of the Supreme Court and the Courts of Appeal (under the Supreme Court’s supervision of the Official Reports) |
| Kind | Style manual |
| Official recognition (court rule) | [California Rules of Court, rule 1.200](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/one/rule1_200) |

Government Code sections 68900–68902 (local index) appoint the Reporter of Decisions and provide for the official reports:

- **68900** — The Supreme Court appoints the reporter of the decisions of the Supreme Court and of the courts of appeal.
- **68902** — Opinions the Supreme Court deems expedient are published in the official reports under the general supervision of the Supreme Court.

Rule 1.200 requires citations in documents filed in the courts to follow either the California Style Manual or The Bluebook, used consistently. A free full-text posting of the Style Manual on `courts.ca.gov` was not found; the Judicial Branch site names the manual in rule 1.200 and incorporates the fourth edition by reference in Official Reports solicitation materials on that host.

### 4. Office of the Law Revision Counsel

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Detailed Guide to the United States Code Content and Features |
| Publisher | Office of the Law Revision Counsel, U.S. House of Representatives |
| Kind | Style manual (editorial / Code style guidance) |
| Official URL | [uscode.house.gov/detailed_guide.xhtml](https://uscode.house.gov/detailed_guide.xhtml) |

The guide explains current editorial styles and policies used to produce the United States Code (section designation, translations, notes, and related features). Related OLRC pages on the same host describe positive-law codification and the Office’s statutory functions; they are process explanations, not a separate drafting manual for new legislation.
