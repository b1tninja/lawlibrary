## How to read the table

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Roster site | Website printed in the 2026 agencies PDF (normalized to `https` where obvious) |
| Publishes a code? | Whether the body posts a compiled regulation book, manual, or law guide on its own host, or only points at OAL/Westlaw / has no APA code |
| Format | Best shape found on a government host this project will crawl. Rank: XML → plain text → Word → HTML → PDF → Westlaw CCR only → none |

## Notes on standout hosts

### CDSS (Social Services) — Word, not a parser target here

CDSS publishes the MPP and its Title 22 department manuals as chapter **`.docx`** files under `/Portals/9/Regs/` (e.g. EAS, CWS, Adoptions, Community Care Licensing). The Regulations Home page states the MPP is published in-house and that the official CCR print/online product is Barclays/Westlaw. Word outranks PDF for reading, but it is **not** XML or plain text. **No parser** under the roster gate.

### CDTFA — HTML law guide

The Business Taxes Law Guide reprints Title 18 sales/use and related regulations as **HTML**. Preferable to PDF for browsing; still not XML/plain text. **No parser.**

### State Fire Marshal / State Lands MOTEMS — Title 24

OSFM Title 19 development and rulemaking are PDF; compiled Title 19 is Westlaw CCR. Title 24 Parts 2 and 9 (and SLC’s MOTEMS Chapter 31F inside Part 2) follow [title-24.md](../title-24.md): no government bulk file; do not crawl ICC.

### DCA boards (BSIS, speech, pest, veterinary, BVNPT, …)

Typical pattern: statute PDF or HTML landing page, then deep links into `govt.westlaw.com` for Title 16. **Westlaw CCR only** for the compiled regulations. No parser.
