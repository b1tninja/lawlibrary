# California Roster 2026 — agencies G–L

Official code publications for State Agencies, Departments, Boards, and Commissions whose roster names start with **G** through **L**. Source: Secretary of State [California Roster 2026](https://www.sos.ca.gov/administration/california-roster/), section *State Agencies, Departments, Boards, and Commissions* (`admin.cdn.sos.ca.gov/ca-roster/2026/state-agencies.pdf`). Checked 2026-09-24 by reading that PDF and the official sites named there. Do not invent regulation text. Do not crawl `govt.westlaw.com`, Barclays, ICC, or Lexis. Do not commit PDFs into git.

XML outranks HTML and PDF. A PDF is not an edition. If the only compilation is the OAL Official CCR on Westlaw, say so and add no parser.

## Verdict

**40 bodies listed. Parsers added: 0.**

No official government-hosted **XML** or **plain-text** compilation of any G–L agency’s regulations was found. What exists is department **PDF** law books and rulemaking packages, occasional **HTML** section pages (DIR Title 8), and pointers to the **OAL Official CCR on Westlaw**. None of those shapes get a package under `us/ca/regulations/`.

## Notes

- **OAL Official CCR.** [oal.ca.gov/publications/ccr/](https://oal.ca.gov/publications/ccr/) points the free online Official CCR to Barclays / Thomson Reuters (`govt.westlaw.com`). That is not a source for this project. No OAL bulk XML or plain-text dump of the CCR was found.
- **DIR Title 8 HTML** is the strongest non-Westlaw presentation in this letter range. It remains HTML (one URL per section), not an XML or plain-text edition, so no parser.
- **DOJ “Law Enforcement Code Tables”** offer `.xml` / `.csv` / `.txt` downloads. Those are LiveScan citation/code-table files, not a compilation of Title 11 regulations.
- **Legislative Counsel pubinfo** is the California *statutory* codes corpus already in lawlibrary. It is listed here because the roster includes the office; it does not justify a regulations parser under `us/ca/regulations/`.
- The roster prints **Land Use and Climate Innovation** twice (current `lci.ca.gov` and former OPR `opr.ca.gov`). Both rows are kept.
