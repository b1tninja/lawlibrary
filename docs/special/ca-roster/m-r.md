# California Roster 2026 — agencies M–R

Source: Secretary of State [California Roster 2026](https://www.sos.ca.gov/administration/california-roster/), section [State Agencies, Departments, Boards, and Commissions (PDF)](https://admin.cdn.sos.ca.gov/ca-roster/2026/state-agencies.pdf). Checked 2026-09-24 by opening each body’s official site (roster website when given) for a **compiled** regulations file. XML outranks HTML and PDF. A PDF is not an edition. Do not crawl `govt.westlaw.com`, Barclays, ICC, or Lexis. Do not invent regulation text. Do not commit PDFs into git.

Background and ranking rules: [california-agencies.md](../california-agencies.md). Authority for OAL’s CCR duty: [authority.md](../authority.md).

## Verdict

**Parsers added: none.** Across the M–R roster bodies below, no official bulk **XML** or **plain-text** (or Word) compilation of that agency’s regulations was found on a government host this project will crawl. What exists is:

- OAL’s Official CCR on Barclays/Westlaw (not a source)
- Department PDF lawbooks / rulemaking express-terms PDFs
- Occasional HTML section browse (still HTML, not an edition shape)
- Statute material already covered by Legislative Counsel pubinfo (do not re-parse from agency reprints)

## Notes

- DCA licensing boards in this range (Medical, Optometry, Osteopathic, Pharmacy, Physical Therapy, Physician Assistant, Podiatric, Psychology, Registered Nursing, Respiratory Care, Occupational Therapy, Naturopathic, Fiduciaries, Engineers/Surveyors/Geologists, BPPE) follow the same pattern: practitioner **PDF** lawbooks or Westlaw deep-links for Title 16 (and kin), plus per-package rulemaking PDFs. None offered bulk XML or plain text of the compiled CCR chapter.
- DIR Title 8 HTML for the Occupational Safety and Health Standards Board and Appeals Board matches the DLSE Title 8 finding in [california-agencies.md](../california-agencies.md): useful browse, not an indexer edition.
- Housing and Community Development is roster letter **H** (already surveyed as PDF + Westlaw in [california-agencies.md](../california-agencies.md)); it is not re-listed here.
