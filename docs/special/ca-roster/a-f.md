# California roster A–F (2026)

Letter range of the Secretary of State [California Roster 2026](https://www.sos.ca.gov/administration/california-roster/) section **State Agencies, Departments, Boards, and Commissions** ([state-agencies.pdf](https://admin.cdn.sos.ca.gov/ca-roster/2026/state-agencies.pdf)). Checked 2026-09-24. Bodies are listed in roster order. Legislature-only bodies are not in this section.

## Method

- Official site: roster `Website` / `Web site` line when present; otherwise a ca.gov host found for that body.
- **Code publication:** whether that body posts its own compiled regulations outside the OAL Official CCR.
- Preferred shapes: XML-like, then plain text or Word. HTML and PDF are noted but are **not** an edition for a new parser. Do not crawl `govt.westlaw.com`, Barclays, ICC, or Lexis. Do not commit PDFs into git.
- **Authorizing note:** only when the roster blurb or the agency site states it. Empty otherwise.
- Already decided: Office of Administrative Law has **no** government bulk XML of the CCR.

## Verdict

**99 bodies** in A–F. **Parsers added: 0.** No official government-hosted **XML** or **plain-text** compilation of an agency’s regulations was found in this range. Most APA regulations are in the OAL Official CCR on Westlaw. A PDF URL list is not an edition.
